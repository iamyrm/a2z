01Who we are

A2Z Riders Hub is a motorcycle gear and parts catalogue based in Biratnagar, Koshi Province, Nepal. For data protection purposes we are the controller of the personal information described below.

Questions about this policy go to info@a2zridershub.com, or use any contact method on the contact page.

02What we collect

This website has no registration, no checkout and no server-side database behind it. Everything we receive reaches us because you chose to send it:

  • Enquiry details — name, phone or WhatsApp number, the size, model or product you asked about, and whatever else you put in the message.
  • Contact form fields — name, phone, category, size or model and message. The form hands your text to WhatsApp on your device; it is not stored on this site.
  • Order details — once you buy, we keep the item, price, date and how you paid for warranty and accounts purposes.
  • Technical logs — your hosting provider may record IP address, browser, requested page and timestamp for security and error handling. We do not link that to you.

We do not receive payment card numbers (there is no online payment), account passwords (there are no accounts), or precise location.

03How we use it

  • To answer your question about stock, size, fitment or price.
  • To prepare and confirm an order you asked us to source.
  • To handle warranty, exchange and repair claims.
  • To keep basic business records required in Nepal.
  • To protect the site from abuse and diagnose faults.

The legal basis is your request (steps taken at your request before a contract) or our legitimate interest in running a shop. We do not build profiles of you and we do not send marketing unless you asked for it — and you can stop it at any time by replying STOP or messaging us.

04Cookies & tracking

This site sets no cookies, no local storage entries for advertising, and no analytics or advertising pixels. Nothing you do here is tracked across other websites.

Filters, sorting and pagination are written into the page address (catalogue.html?cat=helmets&page=2) rather than into storage — that is why you can bookmark a view, and why clearing your browser data changes nothing here.

05Third-party services

To show the catalogue we load resources from a handful of providers. Each one receives your IP address and browser details as part of the request:

  • Google Fonts — the typefaces used on this site are served from Google's servers.
  • Pexels — product and lifestyle photography is delivered from the Pexels image CDN.
  • Google Maps — the embedded map on the contact page loads from Google and may set its own cookies.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — tapping a WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp with your message; Meta processes that under its own terms. Your conversation is also covered by WhatsApp's encryption policy.
  • YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok — social links open those services directly; they only receive data once you follow the link.

We do not control these services. Their own privacy policies apply from the moment you leave this site.

06How long we keep it

  • Unanswered enquiries: deleted after 24 months, or sooner on request.
  • Order and invoice records: kept as long as Nepalese tax and accounting rules require, typically 5 years.
  • Warranty claims: for the life of the warranty plus 6 months.
  • Hosting logs: rotated by the host, usually within 30–90 days.

WhatsApp threads live in your account and in ours; deleting a thread on your side does not delete it on ours, so ask us directly if you want a conversation removed.

07Sharing & transfers

We never sell or rent personal information. We share it only where the job requires it:

  • courier partners who need your name, number and address to deliver;
  • manufacturers or distributors, strictly to process a warranty claim;
  • our accountant or auditor, for records we are required to keep;
  • authorities, when the law obliges us to.

Some of these providers sit outside Nepal, which means your message may be processed in another country. We only use services that apply comparable safeguards.

08Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • show you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct anything inaccurate or out of date;
  • delete information we no longer need to keep;
  • stop sending you messages, or restrict how we use your details;
  • provide a portable copy of what you gave us.

Send the request to info@a2zridershub.com from the address or number we already have on file. We reply within 14 days. If you are unhappy with the answer you can raise it with the relevant Nepalese authority, but we would rather fix it directly.

09Children

This catalogue is aimed at licensed riders and adults buying gear. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us details, tell us and we will remove them.

10Changes & contact

If how we handle information changes, this page is updated and the date at the top is revised. Continued use of the site after a change means you have seen the new version.

Read how trading works in the Terms & Conditions, or ask us anything directly.
