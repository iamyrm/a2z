06Returns, exchanges & warranty

Because safety equipment is hygiene- and integrity-sensitive, returns are narrow. We would rather get the fit right the first time — ask before you commit.

  • Faulty on arrival — return within 7 days with proof of purchase for repair, replacement or refund.
  • Wrong item supplied — we correct it at our cost, including courier charges.
  • Change of mind — accepted within 7 days only if unused, unmounted, in original packaging with all tags and inserts.
  • Not returnable — worn, washed or fitted items; opened consumables (oil, filters, adhesives, visor inserts); clearance or final-sale items; helmets that have been worn or had a strap fitted.

Manufacturer warranty covers manufacturing defects for the period stated on the product page (typically 12 months). It does not cover crash damage, normal wear, unauthorised modification or damage from incorrect fitment. We handle warranty claims with the manufacturer on your behalf.

07Safety equipment notice

Protective gear reduces risk. It does not remove it. No helmet, jacket, glove or armour can prevent injury in every crash. Certification ratings describe laboratory performance, not a guarantee of safety for any individual incident.

Replace any item after a single impact, however light it looks, and replace helmets within the manufacturer's service life — usually five years from the date of manufacture. The date code is printed on the shell; ask us and we will read it with you before you buy.

Riding on public roads in Nepal requires a valid licence, registration and the protective equipment mandated by law. Compliance is your responsibility.
